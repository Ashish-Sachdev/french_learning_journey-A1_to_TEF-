## Accents

1. Aigu (é)
	- Makes "e" sound like "ayy" in "say, day, play"
	- Examples: 
		1. été - Summer (M)
		2. Médecin - Doctor (Unisex) (==Both La and Le can be used== depending on the context and which doctor are we talking about)
		3. Éleve - Pupil/Student (M)
		4. Café - Cafe/Coffee(M)
		5. écouter - To listen 
		6. Marché - Market (M) -- note: **Marche*** mean to *Walk
		7. Fée - Fairy (F)
		8. Clé - Key (F)
		9. Dé - Dice(M) -- note: **De** means *From/Of* pronounced as "duh" 
2. Grave (è,à,ù)
	- Makes "e" sound like *eh* like when you are coughing in style 
	- **à, ù** remain the same  * note: **a, à** - *has, to* **la, là** - *The, There* same prounication
	- Examples: 
		1. Très - Very
		2. Père, Mère - Father, Mother
		3. Problème - Problem (M)
		4. Après - After 
		5. Près - Near
		6. Dès - From * Note: This is similar to work **de
		7. Accès - Access (M)
		8. Collège - College (M)
		9. Succès - Success (M)
3. Circonflexe (â, ê, î, ô, û): *Chapeau - Hat (M)*
	- Indicates the historical missing letter **S**. Affects pronunciation slightly, makes vowel longer.
	- Examples: 
		1. Forêt - Forest (M)
		2. Hôpital - Hospital (M)
		3. Fête - Party (F)
		4. Hôtel - Hotel (M)
		5. Prêt, Prête(F) - Ready (M)
		6. Côte - Coast (F)
		7. Mûr, Mûre - Mature/ Ripe
		8. Sûreté  Assurance (M)
		9. âge - Age (M)
		10. Tâche - Task / Assignment (F)
