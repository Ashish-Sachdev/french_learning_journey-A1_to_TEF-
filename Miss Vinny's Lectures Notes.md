## 1. Basic Pronunciation Rules

### Final consonants

French final consonants are **often silent**, but there are important exceptions.

### 🧠 CRFL memory rule

A useful beginner memory aid is **C, R, F, L**: these final consonants are **often** pronounced.

```text
C  R  F  L
↓  ↓  ↓  ↓
Careful! These are often heard at the end.
```

> ⚠️ **Tutor note:** CRFL is a helpful shortcut, not an absolute rule. French has many exceptions, so learn pronunciation with each new word.

### Final **e**

A final **e** is usually silent.

- **table** → **TAHBL** (the final *e* is silent)
- **petite** → **puh-TEET**

# 2. French Accents

## A. L'accent aigu — **é**

### Rule

**é** normally makes a closed **“ay”** sound, similar to the vowel sound in English *say*.

### Examples

| French      | English         | Gender / Type             | Pronunciation  | Note                                                      |
| ----------- | --------------- | ------------------------- | -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **été**     | summer          | masculine — **un été**    | **ay-TAY**     | Both **é** sounds are pronounced clearly.                 |
| **médecin** | doctor          | person noun               | **med-SAN**    | **un médecin / une médecin** depending on the person.     |
| **élève**   | pupil / student | masculine or feminine     | **ay-LEV**     | **un élève / une élève**.                                 |
| **café**    | coffee / café   | masculine — **un café**   | **ka-FAY**     | The final **é** is pronounced.                            |
| **écouter** | to listen       | verb                      | **ay-koo-TAY** | Infinitive verb.                                          |
| **marché**  | market          | masculine — **un marché** | **mar-SHAY**   | Do not confuse with **marcher** = to walk.                |
| **fée**     | fairy           | feminine — **une fée**    | **FAY**        | The final **e** after **é** is silent.                    |
| **clé**     | key             | feminine — **une clé**    | **CLAY**       | Also spelled **clef**.                                    |
| **dé**      | die / dice cube | masculine — **un dé**     | **DAY**        | **de** = from/of and is pronounced approximately **duh**. |

### 🧠 Visual reminder

```text
é  →  “ay”
été → ay-TAY
café → ka-FAY
```

## B. L'accent grave — **è, à, ù**

### Rule

On **è**, the accent usually gives an open **“eh”** sound.

The accents on **à** and **ù** mainly help distinguish words; they do not create the same sound change as **è**.

### Important word pairs

| Without accent | With accent | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **a** | **à** | **a** = has; **à** = to / at |
| **la** | **là** | **la** = the; **là** = there |
| **ou** | **où** | **ou** = or; **où** = where |

> 🗣 **Pronunciation:** In each pair above, the accented and unaccented versions are usually pronounced the same.

### Examples

| French | English | Gender / Type | Pronunciation | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **très** | very | adverb | **treh** | Final **s** is silent. |
| **père** | father | masculine — **un père** | **pehr** | Open **è** sound. |
| **mère** | mother | feminine — **une mère** | **mehr** | Open **è** sound. |
| **problème** | problem | masculine — **un problème** | **pro-BLEM** | Final **e** is silent. |
| **après** | after | preposition/adverb | **ah-PREH** | Final **s** is silent. |
| **près** | near | adverb/preposition | **preh** | Final **s** is silent. |
| **dès** | from / as early as / starting from | preposition | **deh** | Often appears in expressions such as **dès demain**. |
| **accès** | access | masculine — **un accès** | **ak-SEH** | Final **s** is silent. |
| **collège** | middle school / college (context-dependent) | masculine — **un collège** | **ko-LEZH** | In France, **collège** usually means middle school. |
| **succès** | success | masculine — **un succès** | **sook-SEH** | Final **s** is silent. |

## C. L'accent circonflexe — **â, ê, î, ô, û**

### Rule

The circumflex can mark a historical letter that disappeared, often an **s**. In some words it also helps distinguish meaning or can slightly affect pronunciation.

### 🧠 Historical pattern

```text
French              Related English word
forêt                forest
hôpital              hospital
```

The missing historical **s** is easy to notice in these pairs.

### Examples

| French | English | Gender / Type | Pronunciation | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **forêt** | forest | feminine — **une forêt** | **fo-RAY** | Compare English **forest**. |
| **hôpital** | hospital | masculine — **un hôpital** | **oh-pee-TAL** | Compare English **hospital**. |
| **fête** | party / celebration | feminine — **une fête** | **fet** | Common TEF/daily-life word. |
| **hôtel** | hotel | masculine — **un hôtel** | **oh-TEL** | Silent **h**. |
| **prêt** | ready | masculine adjective | **preh** | Feminine: **prête** — **pret**. |
| **prête** | ready | feminine adjective | **pret** | Final **t** is pronounced here. |
| **côte** | coast / rib / slope | feminine — **une côte** | **koht** | Meaning depends on context. |
| **mûr / mûre** | ripe / mature | adjective | **myur** | Masculine **mûr**, feminine **mûre**. |
| **sûreté** | safety / security | feminine — **la sûreté** | **syur-TAY** | Your original note had “Assurance”; **sûreté** is feminine. |
| **âge** | age | masculine — **un âge** | **ahzh** | Final **e** is silent. |
| **tâche** | task / assignment | feminine — **une tâche** | **tahsh** | Do not confuse with **tache** = stain. |

## D. Le tréma — **ë, ï, ü, ö, ÿ**

### Rule

The **tréma (¨)** usually tells you that a vowel should be pronounced separately from the vowel next to it.

### 🧠 Visual reminder

```text
Without separation       With tréma
      oi                    o + ï
      ↓                      ↓
    “wah”              separate vowels

égoïste → é-go-ïste → ay-go-EEST
Haïti   → Ha-ï-ti   → ah-ee-TEE
```

### Examples

| French | English | Gender / Type | Pronunciation | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Noël** | Christmas | masculine — **Noël** | **no-EL** | **o** and **ë** are heard separately. |
| **maïs** | corn | masculine — **le maïs** | **mah-EESS** | **a** and **ï** are pronounced separately. |
| **naïf / naïve** | naive | adjective | **nah-EEF / nah-EEV** | Masculine **naïf**, feminine **naïve**. |
| **Haïti** | Haiti | place name | **ah-ee-TEE** | Usually used without an article: **à Haïti**. |
| **Israël** | Israel | place name | **ees-rah-EL** | Usually used without an article: **en Israël**. |
| **égoïste** | selfish | adjective, same form M/F | **ay-go-EEST** | **Il est égoïste / Elle est égoïste.** |
| **coïncidence** | coincidence | feminine — **une coïncidence** | **ko-ahn-see-DAHNS** | The **o** and **ï** are separated. |
| **ambiguë** | ambiguous | feminine adjective | **ahn-bee-GOO** | Masculine: **ambigu**. The **ë** itself is not pronounced as a separate “eh” sound here. |
| **aiguë** | acute / sharp | feminine adjective | **ay-GOO** | Masculine: **aigu**. |
| **héroïne** | heroine | feminine — **une héroïne** | **ay-ro-EEN** | Do not merge the vowel sounds around **ï**. |

### Example sentences

**French:** Il est parfois égoïste, mais il essaie de penser aux autres.

**English:** He is sometimes selfish, but he tries to think about others.

**🗣 Pronunciation:** **eel eh par-FWA ay-go-EEST, meh eel eh-SAY duh pahn-SAY oh-ZO-truh**

## Le Passé recent.

>Usage: It is used to express something that just happened

In English, we use the construction have just + past participle, while in French we use the conjugated form of the verb venir

*Conjugation:* Venir(Present) + de + Infinitive verb
eg:
1.  I just saw luc - Je viens de voir Luc
2. He just arrived - Il vient d'arriver
3. We just prepared 
4. Il a appris la nouvelle il y a un instant - Il vient d'apprendre la nouvelle
